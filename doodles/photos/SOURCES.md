Pexels, and its licence was checked on the photo's own page the same day.
Both licences allow commercial use without attribution; attribution is given
here anyway. Unsplash+ (paid, `plus.unsplash.com`) images were excluded.

## Sources that could not be used

- **Pixabay** and **Musopen** answer HTTP 403 (Cloudflare bot challenge) from
  this environment, so nothing was taken from them directly. The light-bulb
  photo above is the Pixabay account's upload *on Pexels*, under the Pexels
  licence.
- **Wikimedia Commons** was searched for object photographs and for a
  recording; it holds no orchestral recording of *L'apprenti sorcier*, and
  its object photos were not needed once Unsplash and Pexels were reachable.
